How to read

Height = number of sports in the intent (few at the top, many at the bottom).
Reduced labelling: sports at their attribute concept μ(m), nations at their object concept γ(g).
Selecting sports B marks every concept whose intent contains B (below (B′, B″)).
Selecting nations A marks every concept whose extent contains A (above (A″, A′)).
